Vecsy — Free Online SVG Editor in Your Browser

Free online SVG editor. Edit layers, colors, text and shapes in your browser, then export SVG, PNG or WebP. No install or account; your files stay on your device.

Enable JavaScript to use the editor. You can read the product information and guides without it.

See every layer

Select, rename, reorder and group. Align several shapes or hide one for a moment. Everything has its place.

Find your colors

Fine-tune fills, strokes and opacity with a live preview. Make small adjustments and see the difference as you go.

Stay close to the source

Switch between the canvas and SVG source. Read highlighted markup, edit directly, or format it with a click.

Export, your way

Keep your work as SVG, or export PNG and WebP. Choose the scale, export selected layers and optimize SVG size.
